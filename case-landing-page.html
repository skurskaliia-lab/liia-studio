Landing Page(04)

About the project
ClientCeres — concept
Year2025
TypeWeb, Landing page

A meal-kit service that plans your plate around your cycle — designed to earn trust first.

A landing page concept for Ceres — a meal kit service that personalises weekly deliveries to the phases of the menstrual cycle.

The design draws on a clinical, data-driven aesthetic: a structured typographic hierarchy, a restrained palette and section layouts that mirror the visual language of medical and scientific platforms. It positions Ceres not as a typical wellness brand, but as a nutritionist-backed service grounded in how the body actually works.

The primary audience is women, with room for broader campaigns aimed at health-conscious households and partners invested in shared wellbeing.
