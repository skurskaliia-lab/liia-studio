Liia

My name is Liia, I’m a graphic designer with a focus on marketing, based in Brno, Czechia. I started as a product photographer — and once I learned Photoshop, everything took off from there.

Over time I expanded into illustration, campaign creatives, social media design, branding and web design. I approach every project with a holistic mindset: not just the single visual, but how each element works for the whole product and the user’s experience.

If you have an idea, product or service to share, I’m here to package it beautifully and present it in a way that captures your audience’s attention.
