SMM Design(01)

About the project
ClientBusiness learning platform · Lecture hall · English school
Year2025
TypeSocial, Visual system

Three social media systems, each built to make content feel like the brand, not like a template.

A social media visual system for a business learning platform offering practical courses for working professionals. The goal was to make educational content feel credible and approachable rather than corporate or academic.

For a philosophical lecture hall I translated abstract, slow-paced subject matter into a quiet, editorial language: muted tones, generous whitespace and a two-typeface pairing, with templates for quote posts, lecture announcements and short thought prompts.

For an English language school for women I built a bright, friendly identity with templates for vocabulary tips, student testimonials and course promotions — playful colour, balanced by clear, legible typography.

  • Announcement system
